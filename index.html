About Me

Hello everyone! My name is Yulin Bai, and I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Geographic Information Science and Technology at USC. I previously earned my bachelor's degree in Measurement and Control Technology and Instruments, which gave me a foundation in engineering, programming, sensors, and data analysis.

During my graduate studies at USC, I have gained experience with GIS, spatial analysis, and geospatial programming. I have worked with ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Online, Python, and ArcPy on different projects. I also have experience with remote sensing and geospatial data through an internship involving remote sensing, GIS applications, and AI-based tools.

Interests and Goals

I am especially interested in the combination of GIS, remote sensing, artificial intelligence, and programming. I hope to explore how satellite and aerial imagery can be processed, analyzed, and integrated with other spatial data for environmental monitoring, urban analysis, autonomous driving, and smart cities.

Through this course, I hope to strengthen my understanding of remote sensing methods and gain more practical experience with image processing and interpretation. I also look forward to learning how people from different backgrounds apply remote sensing to real-world problems.

  • GIS & spatial analysis
  • Remote sensing
  • AI & geospatial programming
  • Environmental and urban applications
