Open full-screen application ↗

Start exploring: choose a room type, switch on the price filter and enter a minimum and maximum. Active filters work together. Scroll the filter panel for availability, ratings and recent reviews. Use “Reset all filters” to restore defaults.

Read the map: click a listing to see its nearest named attraction, approximate distance and attraction count within 3 km. Use Map Layers and Legend in the upper-right controls. Expand the bottom table to browse and sort the filtered records.

Interpret carefully: available nights describe the calendar at the snapshot date, not actual occupancy. Reviews indicate review activity, not bookings. Missing price or rating values are preserved and excluded only when the relevant numeric filter is active.

Questions the map can answer

For travelers: which room types fit a budget near named attractions? How do guest ratings, recent reviews and minimum-stay requirements compare?

For prospective hosts: where are similar listings concentrated, and how do their advertised prices and review activity differ? These are descriptive comparisons; the map does not estimate profitability or verify permission to operate.

For research: how are advertised price, room type and attraction proximity associated? The data do not contain guest incomes, so they cannot establish the preferences of income groups.

Data and measurement limits

The Airbnb dataset is the Inside Airbnb Los Angeles snapshot from 15 June 2026. Its coverage extends beyond the City of Los Angeles, including coastal and northern county communities and Catalina Island. The project is a Los Angeles regional case study, not a complete statewide inventory.

OpenStreetMap features were retrieved on 2 October 2026 Pacific Time for named attractions, museums, galleries, viewpoints, zoos and theme parks. Distances are straight-line approximations using WGS84 coordinates; listing positions are anonymized and some attraction positions are polygon centers. They are not walking or driving routes. Coverage and tagging are uneven, and the two sources have different snapshot dates.

There are 5,565 listings without a nightly price and 11,257 without a guest rating. No values were invented to fill these gaps. Calendar unavailability may reflect reservations or host blocking. Guest income, actual occupied nights and actual revenue are not observed.
